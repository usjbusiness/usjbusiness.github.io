Membership

Join the USJ Business Society.

Membership is free and open to University of Saint Joseph undergraduate students of every major. No business background is required, and there is no selection process.

  1. 1Fill out this short form with your USJ email.
  2. 2The Executive Board confirms that you are a current USJ undergraduate. This is only an eligibility check. Every eligible student is accepted.
  3. 3You are added to our member roster and hear from us at your USJ email.

Questions? Message us on Instagram @usjbusiness or email tcwilliams@my.usj.edu.
